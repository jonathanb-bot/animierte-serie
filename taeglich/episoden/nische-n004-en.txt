// "How to build …" test (MASTER 3b, art: bau, idea no. 31). Chain of "But … So …", ending "Congratulations, you just built …".
// Issue #41: no @klang studio (no EQ/compressor on the voice).
// Sources: Femern A/S, Fehmarnbelt tunnel fact sheet, Nov 2025 (18 km, 79 standard elements of 217 m and 73,000 t, trench avg. 12 m deep
//   and 100 m wide, 15 million m³ excavated, factory near Rødbyhavn, bulkheads make elements buoyant, gravel/sand backfill and stone layer,
//   train 7 min, ferry 45 min); Arup 2026 (longest immersed tunnel in the world, more than 3× the 5.8 km Transbay Tube);
//   Trelleborg (GINA rubber gasket) and immersed-tunnel method: water in the joint is pumped out, outside water pressure compresses the gasket;
//   ballast water in tanks to sink the element. 73,000 metric tonnes → said as "tons" (US viewers), number unchanged.
